# Outdated or wrong passport fee figures you may see (KES 4,550, 6,050, 12,050)
KES 4,550 is the pre-March-2024 price of the 34-page passport. It still appears on a stale official legacy eCitizen help page and on Wikipedia, but budgeting on it leads to underpayment — the current 34-page price is KES 7,550. KES 6,050 and KES 7,550 were the pre-2024 prices of the 50-page and 66-page booklets respectively. KES 12,050 for the 66-page booklet is an error that appears in aggregator guides and even on a stale DIS fees page — the gazetted base is KES 12,500, so the payable eCitizen figure is KES 12,550 (12,050 was actually the pre-2024 lost-passport replacement fee, which likely seeded the mix-up). The separate cheap "East African passport" (KES 990) no longer exists as a category. If a price you have seen disagrees with the eCitizen payment step, trust the payment step.

# Why passport prices seem to disagree — booklet sizes and availability changes
Passport prices differ by booklet size (34, 50, or 66 pages), and the booklet menu on eCitizen has changed several times: the 34-page was withdrawn in July 2025, the 50-page was removed around February 2026 (briefly leaving only the 66-page at KES 12,550 as the cheapest option), and the 34-page returned around June 2026 as the only selectable option. The gazetted prices themselves have not changed since 1 March 2024. This availability churn — plus stale official pages still showing pre-2024 prices — is why different sources quote different prices for "a passport".
