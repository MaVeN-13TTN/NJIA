# Passport fees
The standard 34-page passport costs KES 7,550. Higher fees apply for larger booklets and special categories. The widely-remembered KES 4,550 figure is the pre-March-2024 rate and is outdated — budgeting for it leads to rejected applications. Payment is via M-Pesa, Airtel Money, card, or online banking through the eCitizen gateway. Two payment invoices are issued and both must be downloaded and printed.

# Processing timelines
Normal processing takes roughly 10 working days from biometric capture. Express processing (2-3 days) is available at Nyayo House.

# Processing centres
Nine centres serve applicants: Nairobi (Nyayo House), Kisumu, Mombasa, Eldoret, Nakuru, Embu, Kisii, Kericho, and Bungoma. Diaspora applicants use the nearest Kenyan High Commission or Embassy. Applicants near Thika would typically book Nairobi (Nyayo House).

# Documents for a first-time adult applicant
Original birth certificate and copy. Original national ID and copy. Recent passport-size photograph with white background taken within the last six months. Printed eCitizen application form and both payment invoices. Name discrepancies between national ID and birth certificate cause significant problems and should be resolved before applying.

# Application flow
Register or log in on eCitizen at dis.ecitizen.go.ke, go to Directorate of Immigration Services, New Applications, complete Form 19 and upload documents, pay the fee, receive two payment invoices, book an appointment at a processing centre, attend in person for biometric capture, collect the passport once notified.
