# How long does it take to get a passport once biometrics are done?
Fourteen (14) days as stipulated in the service charter.

# What happens if my date of birth or name differs between my ID and birth certificate?
Make the necessary correction by visiting either Civil Registration Services or the National Registration Bureau (NRB) before applying. The primary documents — birth certificate and national ID — must contain the same information.

# What happens if I discover I do not have all requirements on my appointment date?
You can reschedule the appointment to the next available date, or visit the customer care desk at your passport application centre for facilitation on submission and biometrics capture.

# Why must I bring physical documents when I already uploaded everything?
A passport is a security document; physical documents are required for multiple storage of record.

# Can someone else bring a child for biometrics?
Due to child protection laws, the biological parent must apply and present the child for biometrics, unless one has legal guardianship from the courts.

# What is a consent form?
Authorization by the biological parent or legal guardian to the Directorate for a passport to be issued to a minor.

# Can I renew or replace my passport outside the country?
Yes, through consular services at Kenya High Commissions and Embassies.

# How do I track the progress of my passport?
Log in to your eCitizen account, or SMS your application number (on your receipt) to 22222.

# Do I need a certified recommender's ID copy?
A recommender's ID copy is no longer a requirement.

# Can I collect my passport from a different centre than where I applied?
No. A passport is collected from the passport centre where you did your biometrics.

# What do I need to collect my passport when it is ready?
Carry the invoice with a token attached and your original national ID. For a replacement, also bring the old passport booklet.
