# Ordinary passport fees — how much a passport costs (best-supported figures, unverified)
The most consistent recent sources state the passport price as: A Series 34-page KES 7,550; B Series 50-page KES 9,550; C Series 66-page KES 12,050. Replacement of a lost or mutilated passport: KES 20,050. Payment via M-Pesa, Airtel Money, card, or online banking through the eCitizen gateway.

# Known conflicts between sources (demo material — do not resolve by guessing)
One source claims the 34-page and 50-page booklets were withdrawn in July 2026, leaving only the 66-page C Series at KES 12,500 — which conflicts with the KES 12,050 figure elsewhere and is uncorroborated. Another source still lists the 66-page passport at KES 4,550, the pre-March-2024 rate. A third lists an obsolete 32/48/64-page structure. The only authoritative answer is the fee shown on the live eCitizen payment step.
